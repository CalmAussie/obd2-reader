Checked each time the engine starts while the app is connected: connect with the ignition on and the engine off, then start the engine. A healthy battery stays above about 10 V while starting.
Leave the phone connected to the adapter overnight (on charge, in range) and the app notes the battery's voltage every 5 minutes, then says whether it held its charge or something may be draining it. The adapter uses a little power itself, so don't leave it plugged in for weeks. Without this, the app stops waiting for the car after 30 minutes.
